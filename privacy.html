Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Wren is a private network built for women's safety. Privacy isn't a section of this app — it's the reason the app is built the way it is. This policy explains what information Wren collects, how it's used, and the controls you have. It's written to be read, not skimmed past.

Wren is operated by Wren Studio Inc. ("Wren," "we," "us"). This policy applies to the Wren iOS app and the joinwren.app website.

What we collect

Account information

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. Through it, we receive your name and email address — or, if you choose Apple's Hide My Email, a private relay address instead of your real one. We also receive an anonymous Apple account identifier used to keep you signed in.

Inside Wren, you're represented by an automatically generated handle and an avatar. Your real name is never displayed anywhere in the app, and other members never see your email address.

Content you create

Posts, comments, bumps, and drafts you write are stored so the app can show them. If you attach a photo to a post, we remove the photo's embedded metadata — including any location information stored in the image file — before it's uploaded.

Location

Location in Wren is optional, and you choose the level:

  • Always: if you allow background location, Wren can check your area even when the app is closed so we can notify you about nearby alerts.
  • While Using the App: location is read only when the app is on screen — to tag a place on a post, center the map on your area, or use the current-location button. You won't receive nearby alert notifications.
  • Off: Wren never reads your device location. You can still use the map and read everything — the map just won't know where you are.

Location attached to a post is the place you chose to tag — not a trail of where you've been. We don't build location histories of members.

Device and usage information

We collect basic app usage analytics — which screens are used, which features are used, your device model and iOS version — to understand what's working and what's broken. If the app crashes, a crash report (device information and technical error data) is sent to our error-tracking provider so we can fix it.

If you enable notifications, we store a push token so Apple can deliver them to your device.

If you contact us

If you email us or send feedback, we keep that correspondence so we can respond and improve Wren.

How we use information

We use the information above to run Wren: to operate your account, show posts and the map, deliver notifications you've enabled, keep the community safe (including reviewing reports of posts or members), fix bugs, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it. That's the list.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information. To anyone, for anything.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not display your real name, email, or precise location to other members.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that make the app run, and only what each one needs to do its job:

  • Apple — sign-in and push notification delivery.
  • Supabase — our database and backend hosting, where account and content data is stored.
  • Mapbox — the map. When the map loads, Mapbox receives standard technical data (like your IP address) needed to serve map tiles.
  • PostHog — usage analytics, as described above.
  • Sentry — crash reporting.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own contractual obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of members or the public — consistent with the safety purpose of this app.

Our data is stored on servers located in the United States.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. When you do, your account, posts, comments, bumps, and photos are deleted from our systems. Residual copies in backups are purged on a rolling basis within 30 days. Some aggregated analytics and crash data that doesn't identify you may be retained.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to member data inside Wren Studio is restricted. No system can promise perfect security, but Wren is designed so that the most sensitive things — your identity and your location — are exposed to as few systems, and as few people, as possible.

Age

Wren is not directed to anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we've collected information from someone under 18, we'll delete it.

California residents

California law gives you the right to know what personal information a business collects and to request its deletion. Everything we collect is described on this page, and deletion is built into the app — Settings, delete account. You can also make any request by emailing us. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, so there's nothing to opt out of. We don't respond differently to browsers' Do Not Track signals because we don't track you in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change meaningfully affects your privacy, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
